# Problem Statement 3 — AI-Powered Career Recommendation and Internship Matching Platform (BPUT)

## Objective

Connect BPUT student academic profiles with tailored career pathways, skill interventions, and verified industry internships through predictive analytics.

## Benefits Across Levels

- **Students**: Receive automated digital profiles, personalized career readiness scores, and targeted training recommendations.
- **Placement Cells**: Gain institutional visibility into department-wise skill gaps, branch trends, and placement metrics.
- **Employers**: Access an AI-shortlisted pool of verified student talent.

## App Structure and Navigation

- **Student Portal**: Houses automated profiles built from resume and social profile parsing.
- **Career Recommendation Engine**: Displays predictive clustering models, career paths, and skill-enhancement courses.
- **Internship Matching System**: Unified job board with dynamic candidate shortlisting and recruiter feedback loops.
- **University Analytics Dashboard**: Visualizes employability trends, skill gaps, and industry demand forecasts with exportable reporting features.

## Data Management and Flow

Centralized database aggregating student academic histories, co-curricular data, and employer job requirements, continuously refined via feedback loops.

## Validation and Access Control

Secure multi-role authentication supporting students, placement officers, and corporate employers.
